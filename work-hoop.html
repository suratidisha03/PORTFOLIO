Product marketing · GTM strategy

Go-to-market for a smart real estate platform

Academic product strategy project

Project introduction

An academic product strategy project imagining a video-first rental discovery platform for students and young professionals, built for a $4T+ US rental market that still runs mostly on static listing photos.

Challenge

Apartment hunting still relies on static photos and keyword filters in a market this large, which meant renters were making a major decision with the least useful format available. The opportunity was product and go-to-market at once: build something worth using, then figure out who would adopt it first.

Strategy

Conceptualized Hoop as a short-form video app for apartment hunting, combining a scrollable video feed with AI-powered natural language search. The goal was to make finding an apartment feel less like scanning a spreadsheet.

Execution

Designed the natural language search flow, the scrollable apartment video feed, an agent portal, and a verified marketplace with built-in monetization. Built the go-to-market plan around independent agents and university communities as the initial wedge, since both groups had a direct incentive to seed listings early.

Product MarketingGTM StrategyUX ResearchMonetization
Reflection

Framing go-to-market around who benefits from acting first, agents who get better leads and students who get a faster search, is what made the launch wedge feel realistic instead of theoretical.
