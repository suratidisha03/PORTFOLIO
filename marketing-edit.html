Featured · Measurement × Content Analytics

YouTube changed what counts as a view.

Starting March 31, 2025, YouTube began counting a Shorts view every time the video started or replayed, with no minimum watch time required, instead of only after a few seconds of engaged viewing. Absolute view counts rose 20 to 30% for most creators overnight, with nothing about the content itself changing.

YouTube didn't retire the old definition; it renamed it. "Engaged views" still exists as a separate metric, and it's still the one tied to monetization and Partner Program eligibility. That split matters for anyone reading a view count as a proxy for reach: the public number and the number that actually pays out are no longer the same measurement.

The change is now extending further. Starting August 24, 2026, YouTube is applying the same first-frame, no-minimum-watch-time counting method to every video format on the platform, not just Shorts. For anyone benchmarking campaign performance against a prior period, the comparison itself just changed shape: a "view" in 2026 is measuring something different than a "view" in 2024 was.

Brand Strategy · Acquisition

What e.l.f. actually bought when it bought Rhode

In August 2025, e.l.f. Beauty closed a $1B acquisition of Rhode, Hailey Bieber's skincare brand: up to $800M at close plus a $200M earnout tied to growth. Rhode was the number one skincare brand by earned media value in 2024, growing EMV 367% year over year, almost entirely without traditional advertising. What e.l.f. bought wasn't a supply chain; it was a way of earning attention at scale that its own engine was finding more expensive to produce every year.

Consumer Psychology · Campaign Breakdown

Rare Beauty's first-ever ambassador, six years in

Rare Beauty launched without a celebrity ambassador and stayed that way for six years: a deliberate choice for a brand built around anti-perfectionism. In July 2026, it named Ella Bright, the breakout star of Off Campus, as its first-ever ambassador for the "Made You Blush" campaign. The casting isn't renting a famous face. It's recognizing a cultural moment early enough to attach itself before it cools.

Analytics · Sponsorship

Market share vs. mindshare at the 2026 World Cup

FIFA's official partners committed mid-eight-figures for a projected $1.8B in tournament sponsorship. But Marriott, an official sponsor, was overtaken in impressions by Airbnb, which isn't a sponsor at all. A paid badge buys the right to show up. It doesn't buy the conversation.
