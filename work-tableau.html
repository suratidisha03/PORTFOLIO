Tableau / Data Visualization / Data Storytelling

Cost of Living & Tax Analysis

Independent data visualization project · Tableau

Interactive dashboard · state comparison · Income-to-Cost ratio · what-if affordability model.

Role: Sourced and organized the underlying data, built the calculated fields, and designed the full interactive dashboard.

01 Question 02 Context 03 Data 04 Method 05 Finding 06 Decision 07 Impact 08 Behind the Work 09 Reflection
01 / Question

Where does income actually go furthest, once cost of living and taxes are accounted for?

02 / Context

Raw income comparisons across states are common, but they miss the other half of the equation: what that income actually buys once housing, food, healthcare, and state tax burden are factored in. This independent project set out to build a tool that could answer the affordability question properly, not just the income question.

03 / Data

State-level data on median income, housing cost, food cost, healthcare cost, and state tax burden, organized into a single model for cross-state comparison.

04 / Method

Built a calculated Income-to-Cost ratio in Tableau to combine income and the major cost categories into one comparable measure, then designed an interactive dashboard around it: a state comparison map, an affordability ranking chart, and a what-if model that lets a viewer adjust income and see how the ratio shifts.

Raw income is a poor predictor of affordability on its own.

Once cost of living and tax burden were factored into the ratio, states that looked strongest on income alone didn't always come out ahead, and states with more modest incomes sometimes ranked better once real affordability was accounted for. The ratio made that gap visible in a way a plain income comparison never could.

06 / Decision

Designed the dashboard so the Income-to-Cost ratio, not raw income, is the default lens a viewer sees first, with the what-if model letting anyone test their own income against any state's cost structure, rather than relying on a single static ranking.

07 / Impact

This is a personal data-storytelling project, not a client or business engagement, so the impact is the tool itself: a working, interactive dashboard that turns four separate cost variables into one usable affordability answer.
