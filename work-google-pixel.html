#CatchYourPixels: a brand repositioning campaign

Academic strategy project

Project introduction

An academic strategy project asking a specific question: how could Google Pixel take real market share from an established leader through positioning alone, without competing purely on specs?

Challenge

Pixel already had the hardware to compete. What it lacked was a reason for someone comfortable in another ecosystem to actually switch. Specs alone don't move people who've never had a reason to question their current phone, so the positioning had to do the work that pricing and feature comparisons couldn't.

Process

Started with an audience targeting analysis to understand who was actually switching phone brands and why, rather than assuming the reasons were obvious. That research shaped a positioning strategy built on the camera as Pixel's clearest point of difference, then translated into two connected creative concepts.

Strategy & execution

Designed #CatchYourPixels as a UGC campaign built around the phone's camera, paired with "The Pixel Effect" as a mini-series, both grounded in the audience targeting analysis of who was actually switching phone brands and why.

Results
25.6%
Projected market share growth

Projected through brand repositioning and an ecosystem bundling strategy designed to make switching feel lower-risk.

Reflection

This project is where I started thinking about positioning as a switching-cost problem rather than a messaging problem: the real barrier usually isn't whether someone likes your product, it's whether the cost of changing feels worth it.
