Content strategy · Design

Content and brand systems across five clients at once

INIT Design Studio · Content Marketing & Design Associate · Sept 2023–Apr 2024

Project introduction

INIT Design Studio works with wellness and lifestyle brands on identity and content. I managed content and campaigns across five-plus client accounts at once, while also designing brand systems from the ground up.

Challenge

Five-plus accounts running simultaneously meant no single client could get the depth of attention a dedicated team would give it. The job was keeping every account's identity distinct and its content genuinely useful, without the volume forcing shortcuts.

Process

Built a repeatable system for moving between accounts: a shared content and SEO framework that flexed to each client's voice, so I wasn't reinventing the process from scratch for every brand. That structure is what made it possible to design full identity systems, like THNX, alongside the month-to-month content work without either one slipping.

Creative direction & execution

Designed brand identity systems, packaging, and print collateral for wellness and lifestyle clients, while running the ongoing content and campaign work each account needed month to month. This included THNX, a skincare and personal care identity that later became my graduation thesis.

Managing that many accounts at once taught me that systems beat willpower. Trying to hold five client voices in my head at all times didn't scale; building a repeatable process for each one did.
