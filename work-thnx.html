01 / Context

THNX was my B.Design graduation project, sponsored by INIT Design Studio: a full brand identity and packaging system for an Ayurveda-rooted skincare concept. Ayurvedic beauty brands often lean on one of two extremes on shelf: overly traditional, or generically "clean" and Western. THNX needed a visual language that could hold both credibility and modern retail appeal at once.

02 / The Question

How do you translate an Ayurveda-rooted skincare concept into a cohesive, shelf-ready brand identity?

03 / My Role

THNX · PACKAGING DEVELOPMENT / GRADUATION PROJECT

Working through print tests taught me how much a color that reads correctly on screen can shift once it's on an actual packaging substrate, a lesson no digital mockup could have given me.

09 / What I Found

The strongest version of the identity wasn't the most literally "Ayurvedic" one, and it wasn't the most stripped-down "clean beauty" one either. It was the version that used traditional cues (botanical motifs, warm natural tones) filtered through a modern, minimal packaging structure, so the brand could read as credible on an ingredient level and competitive on a shelf level.

10 / The Decision

Let heritage inform the visual language; let modern packaging structure carry it to shelf.

Rather than choosing between a traditional or a modern direction, the identity system was built to let one lead the tone (heritage-informed color, typography and motif) while the other led the structure (minimal, shelf-competitive packaging), so neither had to be diluted to fit the other.

11 / Result

A complete 166-page graduation thesis and full brand system, from logo and identity through packaging, stationery, collateral and environmental applications, sponsored and reviewed by INIT Design Studio.

12 / Reflection

THNX is the project that taught me to think in systems, not single visuals: a lesson that carried directly into how I approach marketing today. The next thing I'd want to test is the packaging in an actual shelf-comparison setting against real competitor products, rather than in isolation.
