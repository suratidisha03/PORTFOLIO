Campaign management · Field marketing

Marketing a fintech startup from the ground up

iStash · Marketing Coordinator · Sept 2025–June 2026

Project introduction

iStash is a zero-fee merchant wallet, a digital payments platform trying to convince merchants to switch from what they already knew. I joined as marketing coordinator with no existing content strategy, no consistent brand voice, and a sales pipeline that needed work.

Challenge

Grow the brand, bring merchants onto the platform, and make people understand why iStash was worth using, in a product category where trust is the entire sale.

Insight

The real research happened in the field. Talking to merchants in person surfaced objections that never would have shown up in a survey: what actually made them hesitate, and what actually got them to say yes.

Strategy & execution

Built out an Instagram content strategy alongside static posts, carousels, motion graphics, and promotional flyers. Ran Google and Meta paid campaigns with A/B tested messaging, iterating based on what merchants actually responded to in person.
