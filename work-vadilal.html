01 / Context

Vadilal, an established FMCG ice cream and frozen foods brand, was running paid campaigns across Google Ads and Meta without a consolidated view of how they were performing against each other. Before recommending any changes, the first job was building the reporting that could answer that question honestly.

02 / Marketing Question

Where should targeting and creative strategy change based on campaign performance?

03 / My Role

Built a cross-channel reporting view covering reach, engagement and spend for all 5 campaigns, then compared performance across audience segments and creative executions to isolate where the campaigns were and weren't connecting. I don't have permission to reproduce Vadilal's original ad creative here, so this page presents the analytical framework rather than the ads themselves.

07 / What the Data Said

When reach, engagement and spend were laid out side by side across all 5 campaigns, the clearest pattern wasn't which single ad performed best; it was that some audience segments consistently engaged with the same creative more than others did.

08 / What That Meant for Marketing

That reframed the problem from "which ad is best" to "which audience is this ad actually reaching." Optimization needed to start at the segment level, not the creative level, before any budget or targeting change would be worth making.

09 / Recommendation: From Reporting to Recommendation
Audience

Patterns showed some segments consistently engaging with the same creative more than others, pointing to where targeting needs refinement.

Creative

Engagement differences by message and format indicated which creative direction to carry forward, and which to retire.

Campaign

Reporting learnings should carry into future campaign planning rather than resetting with each new push.

10 / Impact

Delivered consolidated performance reporting and a set of targeting and creative recommendations across all 5 campaigns, giving the team a shared, evidence-based view they hadn't had before.
