01 / Context

Vadilal is an established FMCG ice cream and frozen foods brand in India that runs social content alongside paid campaigns on Google Ads and Meta. As a summer intern, my work sat between the content and the results: designing promotional creative, and helping the team see which content formats were actually earning attention.

02 / Marketing Question

Which content formats should the team make more of?

03 / My Role

Designed 24 promotional creatives for ice cream products and offers, compared content performance across formats on views, and reviewed Google Ads and Meta campaign results with the team to inform creative choices. I did not own campaign reporting or budgets. I don't have permission to reproduce Vadilal's original ad creative here, so this page presents the analytical framework rather than the ads themselves.

07 / What the Data Said

Reels and jingles earned more views than the other formats. Short, music-led video was where the audience's attention was.

08 / What That Meant for Marketing

That shifted the question from "which single post did best" to "which format should we make more of." The format, not just the message, was shaping how many people saw the work.

09 / Recommendation: From Reporting to Recommendation
Format

Make more reels and jingle-led video, the formats that consistently earned the most views.

Creative

Carry the strongest messages from the team's campaign reviews into those formats.

Planning

Keep comparing performance by format over time, rather than judging one post at a time.

10 / Impact

Designed 24 promotional creatives, and the format comparison shaped later creative toward reels and jingles. Only supported results are shown here; no CTR, conversion rate, or revenue figures are available to reproduce.

11 / Behind the Work

No workplace or product photos from this engagement are mine to share, the same confidentiality boundary that keeps campaign figures off this page applies here too. What's shareable is the approach: compare by format, then decide what to make next.
