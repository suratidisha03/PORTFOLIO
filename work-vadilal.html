Campaign analysis · Segmentation

Getting precise about who you're actually talking to

Vadilal Industries · Digital Marketing & Campaign Analyst · May–July 2024

Project introduction

Vadilal is an established FMCG brand in India. As a digital marketing and campaign analyst, I worked on paid campaigns and segmentation analysis for a company that already had scale. The problem wasn't reach, it was precision.

Challenge

An established audience isn't the same as a well-understood one. Campaigns were reaching people, but the targeting logic behind them hadn't been questioned in a while, which meant impressions weren't reliably turning into engagement.

Insight

I spent real time understanding who the customer actually was before deciding how to talk to them, rather than assuming the existing targeting was already right.

Execution

Ran 20+ Google and Meta campaigns, building segmentation analyses and campaign reports the team actually used to make decisions, not just filed away.
